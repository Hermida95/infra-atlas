A ClusterIP Service is only reachable inside the cluster. To call it from your machine:

  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "api.fullname" . }} 8000:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Then, in another terminal:

  curl http://localhost:8000/health
  curl http://localhost:8000/graph | head -c 300

Watch the pods become Ready (readiness probe on {{ .Values.probes.readiness.path }}):

  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }} -w
