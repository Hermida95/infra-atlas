A topology spread constraint says: looking at the Pods matching this selector, grouped by this
node label, the difference between the fullest group and the emptiest must not exceed
`maxSkew`. It is the direct, quantitative form of "spread my replicas out", and it fixed the two
problems with anti-affinity -- it degrades gracefully and it is cheap to evaluate.

**The pair of constraints most production workloads want** is zone spread with
`maxSkew: 1` and `ScheduleAnyway`, plus hostname spread with `maxSkew: 1` and
`ScheduleAnyway`. That gets replicas distributed across availability zones and across nodes
within them, while still allowing the Pod to be placed when the cluster is lopsided -- during a
zone outage, say, which is exactly when you do not want your scheduler refusing to run things.

**`DoNotSchedule` is a deliberate choice with a real cost.** It is the right call when imbalance
is genuinely unacceptable -- a quorum that must not have two members in one zone -- and it means
accepting `Pending` Pods when the topology cannot satisfy it. On a cluster with a node
autoscaler that is often the correct trigger: the pending Pod is the signal that a node is needed
in the empty zone. On a fixed cluster it is a trap.

**Two fields change the arithmetic in ways worth knowing.**
`nodeAffinityPolicy` and `nodeTaintsPolicy` decide whether nodes your Pod could not use anyway
are counted in the buckets -- without `Honor`, a tainted or non-matching node makes a zone look
emptier than it is to you. And `minDomains` lets you require a minimum number of distinct
domains, which is how you say "this must actually be in three zones" rather than "evenly spread
across however many zones currently have nodes".

**Cluster-level defaults exist and are easy to miss.** The scheduler's own configuration can
apply default constraints to every Pod, and managed clusters often ship some. If your Pods are
spreading in a way nobody asked for, `kubectl get pod -o yaml` shows the effective constraints
after defaulting -- which is where that behaviour will be visible.
