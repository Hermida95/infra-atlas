Blue is what is serving. Green is the new version, fully deployed and warmed up, taking no traffic yet. The release is one change at the router: the load balancer target group, the DNS record, the weight.

Rollback is the same switch in reverse, in seconds, with the old version still running and still warm. The price is paying for two full environments during the switch, and a database that both versions have to understand at the same time.
