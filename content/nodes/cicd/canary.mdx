A canary routes a small percentage of requests to the new version and compares the two on error rate, latency and saturation. If the new one looks worse the slice goes back to zero; if it holds, the slice grows until the old version is idle.

It is the only strategy that tests a release against real traffic before that release owns all of it, and it needs the most machinery: traffic splitting, metrics labelled per version, and something that decides. Without the metrics it is just a slow deploy.
