Signing binds an artifact's digest to the identity that produced it. In keyless mode there is no private key to protect: the pipeline proves who it is with its OIDC token, and the signature and certificate are stored next to the image in the registry.

Provenance goes one step further and records how the image was built: which workflow, which commit, which inputs. Together they answer the question a registry alone cannot. Not "is this image here", but "did we build it, and from what".
