A smoke test is not the test suite. It is the two or three checks that prove this deployment, in this environment, is really serving: the health endpoint, one read path, one authenticated call.

It runs as part of the deploy, before traffic shifts or before the job reports success, and its failure is wired to the rollback. A deploy that reports success because the pipeline ended without errors is reporting on the pipeline, not on the service.
