The shape is always the same. On a pull request, a job runs a plan with read-only credentials and posts it as a comment, so the review is of a diff against real infrastructure rather than of the HCL alone. On merge, a second job applies it with write credentials, from a protected environment that requires an approval.

Credentials come from OIDC, so there is no access key to store, and only the apply job can write. The failure this avoids is the classic one: a destroy hidden inside a refactor, approved because nobody read a plan.
