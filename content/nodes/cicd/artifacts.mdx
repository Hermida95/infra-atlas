Jobs are isolated: each one gets its own runner, its own filesystem and nothing from the job before it. Anything that has to travel between them — a built image, a lockfile, a generated index — travels as an artifact.

The artifacts that outlive the run are the ones that matter: the container image in a registry, a wheel on an index, a static bundle in a bucket. Everything downstream should consume one of those by digest, never rebuild its own copy.
