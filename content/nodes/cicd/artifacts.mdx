Jobs are isolated: each one gets its own machine, its own filesystem and nothing from the job before it. Anything that has to travel between them — a lockfile, a generated index, a compiled bundle — travels as an artifact, uploaded by one job and downloaded by the next.

What the pipeline keeps when it ends is a smaller set, with a retention window and a size budget: build logs, coverage reports, and the handle to whatever the registry now stores. Later stages refer to that handle by digest instead of building their own.
