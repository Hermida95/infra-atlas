Continuous integration, continuous delivery and continuous deployment are three different promises. Integration says every commit is built and tested together. Delivery says every build that passes is ready to go to production at the press of a button. Deployment says there is no button.

Most teams want delivery rather than deployment, and the difference between them is exactly the manual gate. Everything else in this stage — environments, promotion, the release strategy, the rollback — exists so that pressing that button is boring.
