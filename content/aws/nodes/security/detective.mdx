Detective is the tool for the hour after a finding. It continuously ingests CloudTrail, VPC flow
logs, GuardDuty findings and EKS audit logs and maintains a **behaviour graph** -- entities
(principals, instances, IP addresses, findings) and the relationships between them, with
baselines of what normal looks like for each.

The questions it answers are the ones an investigation actually asks and a log search answers
badly. What else did this role do in the hour before and after? Is this API call unusual for
this principal, or do they do it every day? Which other resources has this IP address touched?
When did this behaviour start? Every answer comes with a time series against a learned baseline,
so "unusual" has a shape rather than being a judgement.

It is deliberately not a detector. GuardDuty raises the finding; Detective is the pivot from
that finding into context, and from the console you can jump straight from a GuardDuty finding
into the graph around it.

Practical notes: enable it organisation-wide from the same delegated administrator account as
GuardDuty, and accept that there is a two-week baseline period before the behavioural
comparisons are meaningful. Pricing is per GB of ingested events, so it is the kind of thing
enabled in a security account for the whole organization rather than per workload.
