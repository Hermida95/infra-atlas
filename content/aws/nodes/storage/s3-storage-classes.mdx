Every S3 class has the same eleven nines of durability except One Zone-IA, which is the one
stored in a single Availability Zone. Everything else that differs is cost and time.

The shape of the pricing matters more than the headline per-GB number. The infrequent-access and
archive classes trade a lower storage price for three things: a **per-GB retrieval fee**, a
**minimum storage duration** (30 days for IA, 90 for Glacier Instant and Flexible, 180 for Deep
Archive) that is charged even if you delete sooner, and a **minimum billable object size** of
128 KB. Which is why moving a million 4 KB thumbnails to Standard-IA costs more, not less: each
is billed as 128 KB, and reading them pays a retrieval fee every time.

**Intelligent-Tiering** exists for the common case where nobody knows the access pattern. It
charges a small monitoring fee per object and moves objects between frequent, infrequent and
optional archive tiers based on what is actually accessed, with no retrieval fees. For a bucket
of mixed, unpredictable data it is almost always cheaper than a guess, and it is the answer to
most exam questions phrased as "access patterns are unknown or changing".

Retrieval time is the other axis, and it is what the Glacier family is really about. **Glacier
Instant Retrieval** is milliseconds -- it behaves like IA with cheaper storage and a higher
retrieval fee, for archives that are rarely read but needed immediately when they are. **Glacier
Flexible Retrieval** needs a restore request first: expedited (1-5 minutes), standard (3-5
hours) or bulk (5-12 hours, free). **Deep Archive** is standard (12 hours) or bulk (48), and is
the cheapest storage AWS sells.

So the question that picks the class is always the same: *how fast do you need it back, and how
often will you ask?* Hot and frequent is Standard. Unknown is Intelligent-Tiering. Known cold
but must be instant is Glacier Instant. Known cold and hours are fine is Flexible. Compliance
retention nobody expects to read is Deep Archive. Reproducible data where losing a zone is
acceptable is One Zone-IA.
